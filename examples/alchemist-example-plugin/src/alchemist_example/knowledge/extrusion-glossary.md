# Melt temperature

Temperature of the polymer melt measured at the die. Drifts in melt temperature change viscosity and
can show up as gel or surface defects in the finished film.

# Residence time

Time material spends between the feed throat and the die. Quality results reflect conditions one
residence time earlier.
